GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked.

Can I use Chronos AI offline?

Offline AI mode and local model management are planned for Ultimate only. Offline authorization requires a valid server-signed Ultimate licence refreshed online at least every 90 days. The feature also depends on a compatible local model, required files, supported hardware, and release availability. Online providers require an internet connection.

Is Chronos AI free?

A Free edition with basic chat, history, settings, themes, and limited online AI features is planned. Plus and Ultimate are one-time lifetime purchases, while their features become available only as they are completed and released.

Can I download it right now?

The website is ready to connect to a release, but the installer link is intentionally disabled until a real, tested build is published.

Will every feature work on every computer?

No. Supported features depend on the operating system, hardware, installed models, and connected AI providers. Platform-specific support will be documented for each release.

Is Chronos AI a subscription?

Plus and Ultimate are one-time lifetime purchases, not subscriptions. Checkout remains unavailable unless a market and currency have been configured and verified for the merchant account.

What does lifetime access mean?

Plus and Ultimate are one-time purchases for lifetime access to the purchased edition, not recurring subscriptions. Checkout is only available after the market and merchant currency are configured; final terms will be shared before sales open.

Can I restore my purchase?

Sign in with the account used for the purchase and use Restore purchases. The server verifies each transaction with Paystack before refreshing access; an unverified payment never grants an edition.

What happens when new features are released?

Future feature updates are part of the planned Plus and Ultimate edition scopes. Features will become available only as they are completed and released; details may evolve before launch.

What is the difference between Plus and Ultimate?

Plus is planned to add chat organisation, personalization, document tools, export, themes, and Plus updates. Ultimate includes the planned Plus scope plus offline AI, local model management, advanced coding, supported image understanding, voice interaction, advanced memory, project workspaces, and Ultimate updates. These are intended scopes, not claims that the features are already available.
