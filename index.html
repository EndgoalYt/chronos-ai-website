What it is

A quieter place to think.

Chronos AI is an AI assistant for the kinds of questions that don't resolve in a single exchange: drafting and revising, working through a problem, reading something closely, planning, and deciding.

The interface is deliberately plain. Conversations are kept together rather than scattered, and the assistant is expected to state what it doesn't know instead of filling the gap. There is no tier that unlocks the good version.

The project is developed in the open as it matures. Capabilities are documented as they become real, and nothing is described here that isn't available in the build you can use today.

Capabilities

What Chronos does.

Four things the assistant is built around. Anything still in progress is marked as such rather than presented as finished.

01

Conversation that holds a thread

Long-form back-and-forth with the context kept intact, so a question asked ten messages ago still counts. Designed for editing a draft, unpicking an argument, or thinking aloud without restarting the explanation.

02

Local models, where your hardware allows

When a compatible model is available on your machine, Chronos can run it directly — no request leaving your device. Which models work depends on your processor, memory, and the files you have installed; the project documents those requirements rather than guessing.

03

Privacy by default, not by setting

No account, no profile, no telemetry tied to your name. Conversation data stays where you put it. Where an online provider is used, that is stated before the request is made rather than buried in a policy page.

04

Document and media understanding — in progress

Reading longer documents and interpreting images are the next pieces of the assistant. Work on these is under way and they will be documented here only once they work in a shipped build. Until then, assume they are not available.

Local-first

Your machine, when it can.

The default assumption is that your data should not need to travel. Where a compatible local model is installed, requests are handled on your own hardware and nothing is sent anywhere.

Local capability is a function of hardware, not of what you have paid. A machine that can run the model can run it; a machine that cannot will say so. When a task does require an online provider, Chronos tells you before the request leaves, so the choice is yours rather than a side effect.

Runs locally
Where a compatible model is installed and your hardware meets its requirements.
Runs remotely
Only when you explicitly choose an online provider. Disclosed before the request.

The terms

Free, and staying that way.

Chronos AI has no paid plans, no premium tier, and no lifetime purchase. It is not a trial with a gate at the end.

There is no checkout, no subscription, and no account wall between you and the assistant. Nothing in this project is monetised, and the source of that commitment is the project itself rather than a pricing page.

If a capability appears on this site, it is available without payment — or it is explicitly marked as not yet shipped.

Getting started

Three steps, no ceremony.

  1. 01

    Open the assistant

    Chronos runs in the browser, so there is nothing to install before you can try it. It can also be installed as an app from the browser for a standalone window.

  2. 02

    Point it at a model

    Choose a compatible local model if you have one available, or connect an online provider if you prefer. The choice is explicit and can change per conversation.

  3. 03

    Start with something real

    Bring an actual piece of work — a draft, a decision, a problem you have been circling. The assistant is more useful against a concrete problem than a warm-up prompt.
