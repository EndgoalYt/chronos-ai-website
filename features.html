Features

What the assistant actually does.

Each capability below is described at the level it is available today. Where something is still being built, it says so rather than implying otherwise.

Capabilities in detail

01

Long-form conversation

Context is carried across a conversation rather than reset every turn.

Ask a follow-up that depends on something said twenty messages ago and it still holds. Useful for revising a draft, working a problem from several angles, or thinking aloud without re-explaining the premise each time.

Conversations can be kept, named, and returned to. Nothing is discarded because a session ended.

02

Local models

Requests handled on your own hardware, when your hardware can do it.

Chronos can run a compatible model locally, which means the conversation stays on your machine and does not leave it. Whether that is possible is a property of your processor, memory, and the model files you have installed — not of your plan.

If a local model is not viable, the assistant says so plainly instead of quietly falling back to something remote.

03

Privacy and control

No account, no profile, no telemetry attached to a name.

There is nothing to sign in to, so there is no identity to track. Conversation data stays where you put it. When an online provider is used, the request is disclosed before it is sent, so the decision is explicit rather than a side effect of using the app.

04

Writing and editing

Drafting, tightening, restructuring, and arguing with your own text.

The assistant is set up for the unglamorous parts of writing: a paragraph that isn't landing, a structure that has drifted, a section that needs to be half its length. It will push back on a weak argument rather than agree with it.

05

Reading and analysis

Working through material that is longer than a prompt.

Longer documents and detailed questions are handled with the relevant part of the material in view, rather than summarised from memory. The assistant distinguishes what is in the text from what it is inferring.

06

Planning and decisions

Thinking a decision through before committing to it.

Useful when the choice has several reasonable options and the trade-offs matter. The assistant will lay out what is actually at stake, including the parts of the decision that are not technical.
