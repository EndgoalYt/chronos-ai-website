About

An independent project, built to stay free.

Chronos AI is an AI assistant built around three commitments: that the useful version should not sit behind a payment, that your data should not need to leave your machine, and that the assistant should say when it doesn't know.

Why it exists

The reasoning.

Most AI assistants are built to convert attention into a subscription. The parts that make an assistant genuinely useful — context that persists, local models that keep data on your machine, a straightforward interface — are the parts most often reserved for the paid tier.

Chronos takes the opposite position. Those capabilities are the baseline, not the upgrade. There is no tier structure, no trial clock, and no feature held back pending a purchase.

That is a product decision, not a temporary promotion. The absence of payment infrastructure is deliberate: there is no checkout to remove, because there was never one.

Principles

What the project holds to.

01

Free means free

No paid plans, no premium tier, no lifetime purchase, no checkout. No account wall between you and the assistant. If a capability is on this site and marked as available, it is available without payment.

02

Accurate description over marketing

Capabilities are stated at the level they actually work. Work in progress is labelled as such. There are no invented testimonials, no invented statistics, and no claims about performance that have not been measured.

03

Local where possible

The default assumption is that computation belongs on your hardware. Where a compatible model is installed, requests stay on your machine. Remote providers are an explicit choice, disclosed before the request.

04

No identity, no profile

Without accounts there is nothing to profile. Conversation data stays where you put it. The site sets no advertising cookies and loads no third-party trackers.

Status

Where the project is.

Chronos is usable in the browser today and still maturing.

Available now

In development

Not planned

Cost
None. There is nothing to buy.
