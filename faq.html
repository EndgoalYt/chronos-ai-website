FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

If something isn't covered here, the contact page is the right place to ask.

Is Chronos AI free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no paid plan, no premium tier, and no lifetime purchase. There is no trial and nothing held back for later payment.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no sign-in. Nothing on the site asks you to create an identity, and there is no profile kept against your name.

Why is it free? What is the catch?

There is no catch and no monetisation. The project is independent and is not funded by subscriptions, ads, or data. Its cost is the time spent building it, not something recovered from users.

Can I use it offline?

Partly. The application shell can be cached so the interface loads without a connection, but generating a response still requires a model — either a local model on your machine, or a remote provider, which by definition needs a network.

How do local models work?

If you have a compatible model installed and your hardware meets its requirements, Chronos runs it on your machine and the conversation stays there. Whether that is possible depends on your processor, memory, and the model files you have; there is no server-side switch that enables it.

Does using Chronos send my data anywhere?

Not by default. With a local model, nothing leaves your machine. If you choose an online provider for a task, that request is disclosed before it is sent, so the transfer is a decision you make rather than a side effect.

What happens to my conversations?

They stay where the app stores them, on your side. There is no account to sync them through and no server copy created by signing in. Clearing the application's data removes them.

Can I download and install it?

The browser application is available now and needs no installation; it can also be added to your home screen or installed as a standalone window from a supported browser. Packaged desktop installers for Windows, macOS, and Linux are not published yet. When one is released, the download page will link to it directly — no placeholder links are posted.

Which platforms are supported?

Any current browser with a recent version is the baseline. Desktop packaging is in development and will be listed per platform once builds exist and have been tested.

What can it do today?

Long-form conversation with context carried across turns, writing and editing help, reading and working through material, planning and decision support, and local model use where hardware allows. Document and image understanding are in development and not yet part of a released build.

Will features be added later?

Yes, as they are built and tested. Capabilities are documented here as they become real rather than announced in advance. If a feature is not listed as available, it is not available.

Does using Chronos cost anything in API or provider fees?

Chronos itself charges nothing. If you connect a third-party online provider that has its own pricing, that provider's terms apply to those requests. Using a local model avoids this entirely.

How do I report a problem or request something?

Use the contact page, or raise it in the conversation itself. Concrete reports with the model and platform involved are the most useful.
